# Adaptive story arcs via AI

The story arc structure (chronological, emotional, or thematic) is determined by AI based on the feature space rather than being user-selected or fixed. We chose this because memories naturally suggest their own structure — a birthday trip may be best told chronologically, while a collection of moments across years may benefit from thematic grouping. Hard-coding a single approach or asking users to choose would force an artificial structure on what should emerge from the content itself.
